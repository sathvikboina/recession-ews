# Team Plan: One-Week Sprint

Status: **active; last reviewed 2026-11-08.** This replaces the earlier five-week plan. The work is
compressed into **seven days**: Days 1–5 are individual work by four people, and Days 6–7
are shared combining, cross-review and sign-off. A plain-English Excel version is
`Team_Plan.xlsx` (12 sheets, generated by `make team-plan`). Edit
`scripts/make_team_plan.py` and regenerate; do not hand-edit the workbook.

The project is label-first. The USA label is built, validated and frozen as
**`labels-v3`**. The 2026-11-06 data extension moved the panel and reporting window from
2025Q4 to **2026Q3**: 1990Q1–2026Q3, 147 quarters. `labels-v2` and `labels-v1` remain
archived byte-identically. The estimator, BBQ settings, diffusion gate and eight-indicator
structure did not change. No feature table and no model exist yet. The next milestone is
Japan's label (priority) and Germany's coverage/label decision, then a leakage-safe
feature/harness layer, then the first out-of-sample model result. The first model result is
**USA-first**; Japan and Germany join only if their labels pass and time allows. India is
audited separately because it cannot pass the §19 gate.

## The one-week shape

| Day | Mode | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Individual | Source maps/specs; everyone starts from the current repository state |
| Day 2 | Individual | First real build: Japan data, USA features, model interfaces, India downloads |
| Day 3 | Individual | Labels, draft folds, first model runs, India verdict; first hand-off |
| Day 4 | Individual | Germany, final folds, ensemble and headline run, docs/clean-clone |
| Day 5 | Individual | Freeze outputs, write notes, hand off; all four lanes close by end of day |
| Day 6 | Shared | Merge in the fixed order 1 → 2 → 3 → 4; run checks after every merge |
| Day 7 | Shared | Cross-review, clean-clone check, go/no-go; packaging only after approval |

Days are a guide, not a promise. A lane that finishes early reviews its own outputs and
helps test the hand-off; it does not start another lane's work. If a lane slips, the Day 5
hand-off must say what is incomplete rather than hiding it.

## Ground rules for every work package

1. **Do not change the USA label without a new version.** `labels-v1` and `labels-v2` are
   byte-identical in `outputs/labels/archive/`; `labels-v3` is active. Any further change
   needs a new version, a new decision record and a re-validation. The builder now refuses
   to overwrite a frozen label unless the canonical growth digest still matches; use
   `--allow-overwrite` only as an explicit, recorded decision.
2. **Do not add an ensemble after seeing scores.** The ensemble is pre-registered in
   `docs/PROPOSAL_ADDENDUM.md`. The rule is frozen before results.
3. **No leakage.** Walk-forward only; every scaler, selector, model parameter, weight and
   threshold is fitted inside the training fold; the test block is untouched; a held-out
   country contributes nothing.
4. **One active source row per scope key.** Raw downloads are immutable under
   `data/raw/<source>/<run-id>/`; provenance goes in `data/manifest.csv`,
   `data/fred_verification.csv` and `data/series_resolution.csv`; retirements go in
   `data/superseded_series.csv`.
5. **Missing stays missing.** No interpolation, no forward fill, no partial-quarter
   imputation. A country that fails the gate is a finding, not an exception.
6. **Record decisions when made.** Use `decisions_log.csv` and `docs/DECISIONS.md`; do not
   rewrite history after seeing results.
7. **One branch per package.** Open a PR with a short description, the commands run, the
   hashes that changed and the tests that pass. Do not merge a red `make check test`.
8. **Regenerate, do not hand-edit, generated artefacts.** `config/*.yaml` comes from
   `spec.py`; `data/scope.csv` comes from `scripts/scope_catalog.py`; the workbooks come
   from `scripts/make_team_plan.py` and `scripts/make_worklog.py` (`make team-plan`,
   `make worklog` or `make reports`).
9. **No forecast claim before out-of-sample results.** Say "we built and validated a
   recession label". Do not say "we predict recessions".
10. **No bundle, mirror, zip or release tag without explicit approval.** Packaging is a
    Day 7+ decision, not a development step.

## Working style: guardrails, not micromanagement

The rules above protect the result. Inside them, each owner has real freedom.

- **DO** ask questions early, write down assumptions, add tests, commit in small steps,
  keep outputs reproducible, and say when you are blocked.
- **DO** choose your own implementation, plots, diagnostics and code structure if the
  outputs and hand-off contract stay the same.
- **DO** spend up to about half a day (roughly 10% of your lane) learning a tool, writing a
  short explainer or trying a better approach. Note it in your hand-off note.
- **DON'T** change a frozen label, the ensemble rule, the 6-of-8 gate or a pre-registered
  test after seeing scores.
- **DON'T** leak future data or label outputs into the predictor table, hand-edit generated
  artefacts, hide a failed check, or commit restricted raw data.
- **DON'T** silently take over another lane. Ask the owner and record the change.
- **Allowed creative liberty:** you may add extra validation, a small notebook, a figure, a
  glossary, a "what I learned" section, or a cleaner internal interface. If a change alters
  a shared output, discuss it at the stand-up and record it before merging.

One branch per lane. Do not mix lanes. Do not merge a red branch. If a merge moves a frozen
hash, stop and fix the cause; never patch a result table by hand.

### Day 7 — Cross-review and go/no-go

| Step | What happens | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cross-review: Shravani reviews features/folds; Arshiya reviews labels; Palchhin reviews docs/India; Sathvik reviews models. Write findings, not verbal comments. | review notes |
| 2 | Clean-clone check: Arshiya runs the fresh clone, `make check`, `make test`, label hash check and headline reproduction; Sathvik records the evidence. | clean-clone evidence |
| 3 | Gate review: label, features, models and release. Record a clear go or no-go with reasons and follow-up owners. | written decision |
| 4 | Packaging only if approved: Sathvik and Arshiya build the bundle, mirror, zip and release tag. Otherwise write the findings and next actions. | final package or findings note |

### Four gates

1. **Label gate:** a country needs ≥6 of 8 core indicators at ≥70% of the 147-quarter
   window (≥103 quarters), plus a sensible chronology check. Do not relax it.
2. **Feature gate:** no BEAI-derived output, NBER series, future data, country id or
   country-size level in the predictor table. Raw BEAI-eligible activity series are allowed
   only as separate predictor copies with their own transform and lag. Fix the builder; do
   not train on a leaking table.
3. **Model gate:** every model uses the same folds and features; the threshold is locked
   before the test block; the ensemble rule is applied once. The primary result is the USA
   result; JPN/DEU are added only after their labels pass.
4. **Release gate:** clean clone passes, docs agree, and the project lead approves. No
   bundle or tag without that approval.

## Definition of done for the combined project

- USA and Japan have frozen BEAI labels with a chronology comparison; Germany has a frozen
  label or a complete coverage decision.
- India has a downloaded-data coverage verdict (or a recorded access failure) and is
  reported as a case study if it fails 6/8.
- The predictor feature table has no BEAI-derived outputs and passes the leakage tests;
  any allowed raw BEAI-eligible copies are documented as separate predictor copies.
- The USA country × horizon × model result reproduces from a seed; JPN/DEU results are
  reported only if their labels landed.
- The pre-registered ensemble is evaluated exactly once under its rule; a negative result
  is acceptable and reported.
- `make check test` is green; a clean clone of the combined tree reproduces the label
  hashes. The Day 4 dry run on current `main` is not sufficient.
- The write-up uses label-validation wording, states the revised-data caveat, and makes no
  forecasting claim that the out-of-sample results do not support.
- Nothing is bundled, mirrored, zipped or tagged without explicit approval.
