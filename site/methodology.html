Methodology

Build the chronology first. Then protect the test.

The method is deliberately conservative: one frozen estimator, one table, one fold file, and a firewall that is tested by trying to break it.

An ex-post chronology from coincident activity, not a forecasting model.

3 · The walk-forward protocol

Every test quarter is scored once, after everything before it.

folds.json · frozen

Outer training

Expanding, initial 80 quarters. The training block grows by one quarter per fold.

Inner validation

Initial 40 quarters, time-ordered, entirely inside the parent training block.

Purge

Equals the horizon before every test and validation block, so overlapping targets cannot cross the boundary.

4 · Countries and scope

Labels where the data support them; a finding where they do not.

USA

25/25 series resolved. 147-quarter label and predictor table. Primary study.

Japan

6/8 indicators pass. 144-quarter label, frozen under D-R38. 5/5 ESRI episodes matched.

Germany

Strict Q-02 leaves 5/8. No label; delivered as a coverage finding.

India

1/8 official indicators. Label-B coverage case study only; the 6-of-8 gate is not relaxed.

What this method cannot say

The data are revised history with simulated publication lags, not real-time vintages. The label is an ex-post chronology, not a real-time recession call. Four USA episodes make episode-level conclusions fragile.
